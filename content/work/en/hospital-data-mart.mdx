## Context

Academic project in the Business Intelligence engineering degree at ISMAGI, March to April 2025. Audience: a hospital's **medical management**.

## Problem

Consultations are recorded in a transactional (OLTP) system, designed for data entry rather than analysis. Management cannot easily answer questions such as:

- how many consultations per department and per month?
- what is the average duration per doctor?
- what do medical procedures cost per period?

## My role

- Alignment matrix between business goals and metrics.
- Star-schema modelling and data dictionary.
- SSIS ETL development.
- SSAS cube and Power BI reports.

## Architecture

<Architecture />

The star schema has one fact table, **FactConsultation**, and four dimensions: **DimDate**, **DimDoctor**, **DimDepartment**, **DimPatient**. Key measures: number of consultations, consultation duration, cost of medical procedures.

## Technical choices and trade-offs

- **Star rather than snowflake.** Fewer joins, simpler queries and faster reports, at the cost of slight redundancy in dimensions.
- **Grain = one consultation.** The finest level, which supports every requested aggregate.
- **SSAS cube.** Measures are pre-aggregated for fast slicing by department, doctor and period.
- **One Microsoft ecosystem** (SQL Server, SSIS, SSAS, Power BI): native integration, but a single-vendor dependency.

## Outcomes

- Interactive Power BI dashboard for medical management.
- Multidimensional analysis of consultations by department, doctor and period.
- Data dictionary documenting every table and measure.
