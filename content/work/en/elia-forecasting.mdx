{/* Case study — Forecasting electricity flows in Belgium (EN). */}

## Context

End-of-year project at ISMAGI, April to August 2025. Data: cross-border electricity flows published as open data by **Elia**, Belgium's transmission system operator.

## Problem

Belgium exchanges electricity with five neighbours: the Netherlands, France, Germany, Luxembourg and the United Kingdom. These flows change direction and volume throughout the day. Observing them after the fact is not enough: they need to be **anticipated**.

## My role

- Automated data collection from the Elia API.
- Time-series preparation and quality checks.
- Training and comparing forecasting models.
- Designing the interactive dashboard.

## Technical choices and trade-offs

- **Time series first.** An ARIMA model gives an interpretable baseline, with confidence intervals a non-specialist can read.
- **Adjustable horizon and interval.** Users choose the forecast horizon and confidence level instead of receiving a single forecast.
- **Visible data quality.** The dashboard shows the last update and data quality, so everyone knows what each number rests on.
