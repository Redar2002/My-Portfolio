## Problem

A meeting produces decisions, owners and deadlines. Without a reliable report, they get lost:

- reports are written by hand, when they exist at all;
- action items are not tracked from one meeting to the next;
- no metric measures how effective meetings are.

## My role

- Designed the NLP/RAG pipeline in Python.
- Integrated the **Gemini** API to generate summaries, decisions, action items and KPIs.
- Connected tools through **MCP** (Model Context Protocol).
- Built the metrics in **Power BI**.

## Technical choices and trade-offs

- **RAG rather than fine-tuning.** Answers stay grounded in the actual meeting content, without retraining a model. The trade-off: quality depends on how text is chunked and retrieved.
- **Structured outputs.** The model returns defined fields (decisions, actions, owners) instead of free text, so they can be counted and tracked in Power BI.
- **MCP for integrations.** One standard protocol to connect the model to tools, instead of one-off integrations.
